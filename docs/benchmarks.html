The full table

Two regimes, read honestly. Reductions and transcendentals win 3–25×: LLVM can't auto-vectorize FP reductions without fast-math, so the naive baseline stays scalar, and compute-heavy maps are dominated by vectorized polynomial approximations. Trivial elementwise ops (relu, clip, sqrt) sit at ~1× because the compiler auto-vectorizes the baseline too — both are memory-bandwidth bound. erf/erfc have no std baseline: their naive column is the Abramowitz–Stegun 7.1.26 polynomial, a speed reference of a far less accurate algorithm, and the bench distribution is tail-heavy, where lanes pays two correctly-rounded vector exps to hold its accuracy contract (2–3× faster on small/mid-heavy inputs).
